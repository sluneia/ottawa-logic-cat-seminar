Logic and Category Theory Seminar

Department of Mathematics and Statistics
University of Ottawa

The Logic and Category Theory Seminar brings together researchers working in category theory, higher category theory, logic, homotopy theory, type theory and neighboring areas. We also advertise selected talks from related seminars and events,such as the ItaCa Fest and the UOttawa Math + Stat Colloquium, that we think may be of interest to our community. Everyone is welcome.
